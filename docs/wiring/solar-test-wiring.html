Before first connection

  1. Read the ship-code sticker on all four batteries; note any that differ from A-22 / 7-22.
  2. Charge each battery on its own (MotoMaster fixed 2 A or the NOCO G750).
  3. Rest a few hours; resting voltages should agree within 0.1 V. After a day's rest a healthy battery reads 12.8 V or more. Leave out any that don't.
  4. Check the DROK reads at least 12.0 V on a meter, and the polarity at the injector's DC terminals, before connecting the injector. Leave the camera's 12 V barrel unconnected.

Connection order

  1. Fuses out. Build the bank: each battery to the busbars with its own equal-length leads.
  2. Fit the four battery fuses, then the 20 A main fuse.
  3. Battery to the Victron first, and wait for it to start up and detect 12 V.
  4. Set up the Victron in VictronConnect (settings at left).
  5. Wire the panel through the isolator to the PV terminals, then close the isolator.
  6. Fit the load fuses: camera branch (DROK and injector), then Pi branch. Confirm the camera boots on PoE and the Pi sees it.

To disconnect: reverse order. Open the PV isolator first, then pull the main fuse.

One panel will not keep up. A 100 W panel gives about 160 Wh/day on an average December day, enough for about 6.7 W. The system draws about 13 W, so the bank will run down over a few days and the 11.8 V load disconnect will switch it off. That is expected: this test measures harvest and draw. Adding the second panel in series (about 44 V, within the 75 V limit) roughly doubles harvest.
Cold. At −20 °C these batteries give about 50–60% of rated capacity, and a lead-acid battery near half charge can freeze around −24 °C. Don't lower the 11.8 V disconnect for a cold test. The bank weighs about 22 kg.
PoE costs power. The DROK and the injector are two conversions in a row, so expect the camera to draw about 1–2 W more from the battery than on its 12 V input. Measure it: telemetry logs the Victron's load current.
Age. The batteries date from about early 2022, near the end of a typical 3–5 year life. The 72 Ah figure is nominal until the capacity has been measured.
